Page One

A little something I wanted to tell you

a two-minute read

,

There is something I've been meaning to tell you.

And I thought maybe the best way was to simply be honest with you.

When we first met, I honestly didn't know where our conversations would lead.

I wasn't looking for marriage.

I wasn't expecting to meet someone and start thinking about a future together.

But somewhere along the way, that changed.

As we started talking more, sharing pieces of our lives, our experiences, our likes and dislikes, and the things that have shaped us, I found myself looking forward to hearing from you.

I enjoyed our conversations.

I enjoyed listening to your stories and telling you mine.

And somehow, the more I got to know you, the more I started to appreciate the person behind the conversations.

Some connections aren't planned.

They simply grow quietly.

I think what stayed with me most is how naturally we could talk.

About serious things.

About silly things.

About things from our past.

About things we probably don't tell everyone.

We've both lived through experiences that have left their own marks on us.

And perhaps because of that, there is something comforting about being able to talk without pretending that life has always been perfect.

I've started to feel that our conversations have become something I genuinely value.

And somewhere between all those conversations…

I realised that I like you.

Not because I planned for it.

Not because I was searching for it.

It just happened.

And eventually, I started asking myself a question.

What if this could become something more?

There is something more I want to share with you.

Page Two

What I'm really trying to say

about four minutes

So, this is me being completely honest with you.

I don't know exactly when my feelings changed.

Maybe it was through our conversations.

Maybe it was through the little things we discovered we have in common.

Maybe it was simply getting to know you a little more each day.

But I know that somewhere along the way, I stopped thinking of you as simply someone I enjoy talking to.

I started wondering what it would be like to have you in my life for much longer.

And that thought stayed with me.

I know life isn't simple

I also know that neither of us is starting from a blank page.

We both have a past.

We both have experiences that have shaped who we are today.

I have responsibilities that I will always carry, including being a father and being there for my child.

There are circumstances in my life that aren't necessarily simple.

And I don't want to hide any of that from you.

If we ever seriously consider something between us, I want it to be built on honesty, not on pretending that everything is uncomplicated.

But here's what I want you to know

I don't want to promise you that life will always be easy.

I know there may be difficult conversations, complicated circumstances, and days when things don't go the way we hoped.

But if we ever choose to walk this path together, I want you to know something about the way I see it.

I don't want to be there only for the beautiful days.

I want to be the person beside you on the difficult ones too.

When you're happy, I want to celebrate with you.

When you're tired, I want to be someone you can lean on.

When life feels overwhelming, I want us to face it together rather than facing it alone.

What I can promise

I won't expect you to be perfect.

And I don't expect our life together to be perfect either.

I believe two people can have difficult days, misunderstand each other, learn from one another, and still choose to understand each other instead of giving up.

I can't promise that I will always have the perfect answer.

But I can promise that I would want to communicate.

To listen.

To understand.

To work through things.

And to not simply walk away when life becomes difficult.

Your past doesn't scare me

Your past wouldn't make me value you any less.

I know you have your own scars, your own experiences, and your own story.

I have mine too.

Maybe that's one of the reasons our conversations have felt different to me.

Neither of us needs to pretend that life has always been simple.

We already know that life can change people.

What matters to me is who we choose to become from here.

What I would want

If someday you became my wife, I wouldn't want you to feel that you have to carry everything by yourself.

I would want us to build a life where we can:

  • talk honestly,
  • support each other,
  • laugh together,
  • disagree respectfully,
  • celebrate each other's happiness,
  • and keep finding our way back to each other when life gets difficult.

I want to know your good days.

Your difficult days.

Your little joys.

Your worries.

Your dreams.

And even the things you don't always say out loud.

The actual question

Maybe neither of us needs a perfect beginning.

Maybe what matters is whether two people are willing to build something honest, patiently, and together.

So I'm not asking you to have every answer right now.

I'm not asking you to promise me a future today.

I'm simply asking whether you could see the possibility of us.

Whether you would be willing to sit with me, talk openly, and explore the possibility of building something meaningful together.

Because somewhere along the way…

I started wanting that with you.
